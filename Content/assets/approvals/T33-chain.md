## segment_02 (regions 4-6)

Match the art style of the reference image labelled Style reference exactly (outline weight, neon rim light and glow, glossy shading, colour saturation), but draw only the new subject described here, not that reference's layout or objects. The reference image labelled Previous segment is the part of the same world map directly below this one. Continue that same illustration upward from its top edge: the bottom fifth of the new image must be a seamless continuation of the top fifth of the Previous segment, with the same glowing path position and width, the same terrain, water and lighting, so that the two images can be overlapped and blended into one picture. This is a vertical segment of a fantasy adventure world map for a mobile game, seen from a slightly raised angle. One wide glowing neon green path winds from the bottom centre of the image up to the top centre of the image, crossing the bottom edge and the top edge exactly at the horizontal centre. Along the path, from bottom to top, are three distinct regions: Candy Canyon (pink, mint), Bamboo Temple (jade, red) and Frosty Peaks (ice blue, white). Each fills about one third of the height as a detailed landmark scene with its own colours and terrain, flowing smoothly into the next through natural transitions such as water, hills or clouds. Paint it as one single continuous illustration: no panels, no frames, no borders, no inset pictures and no horizontal dividing lines or hard seams anywhere, with each region melting softly into the next, and the left and right edges simply cropped scenery. Keep the path and one small open spot on the path for each region free of any marks.
